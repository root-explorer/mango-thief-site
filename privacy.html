🥭 Mango Thief: privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Mango Thief is a puzzle game. This page says what information the game handles.

What the game stores on your phone

  • Your progress: which levels you finished, your stars, your best number of moves, your daily puzzle results and streak, and your chosen outfit.
  • The moves of the level you are playing, so you can continue if the app is closed.
  • Your sound and vibration settings.
  • A count of how many times you were caught, used only for an achievement.
  • A short technical note if the game hits an error, so the game can recover. It stays on your phone and is not sent anywhere.

This is removed when you uninstall the game, clear its data, or use “Erase progress” in the game's settings.

Google Play Games

If you sign in to Google Play Games, the game:

  • saves your progress to your own Google account, so it can be restored on another phone;
  • unlocks achievements on your Play Games profile.

Sign-in is optional and the game works fully without it. Google handles this information under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. You can delete the saved game data from your Play Games settings.

Sharing

When you tap “Share”, the game hands a short text and picture of your result to your phone's share menu. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app to share with, and the game does not see what you do there.

What the game does not do

  • It shows no ads.
  • It has no analytics or tracking.
  • It does not collect your name, email, contacts, location or photos.
  • It does not sell or share information with anyone.

Children

The game has no chat, no purchases and no ads.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
